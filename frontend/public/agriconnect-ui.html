Simple Farm Weather Guide

🌡️ Temperature Shows heat level. Over 35°C dries flowers and stresses crops. Spraying in temperatures above 30°C causes chemical liquids to evaporate.
💧 Humidity Moisture in the air. Above 80% creates warm damp conditions where fungal leaf diseases and blight spread fast.
💨 Wind Speed Winds under 12 km/h are safe for spraying. Winds over 15 km/h blow chemical droplets away to other fields and waste medicine.
🌧️ Chance of Rain How likely it will rain. Never spray if rain chance is above 40%, because rain washes medicine into soil runoff.
🌱 Water in the Soil Normal is 20% to 30%. Below 15% means crops need watering. Above 35% means soil is wet and needs drainage.
☀️ Water Lost (ET0) Water evaporating from plants and ground. High values (>5 mm/day) mean soil is drying out fast in the sun.

Government Schemes

Find government help for farming, loans, subsidies and crop insurance.

Official Government Information Notice

All schemes listed below link directly to verified official central and state government websites (.gov.in / .nic.in). AgriConnect provides this directory to help farmers find official support, but does not operate or control these government services. Links open safely in a new browser tab. AgriConnect never asks for your Aadhaar card number, bank passwords, or PINs.

Income Support • Central Govt Ministry of Agriculture

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Direct income support money sent straight to farmer bank accounts to help purchase seeds, fertilizers, and farm equipment.

What it helps with: ₹6,000 per year given in 3 equal payments of ₹2,000 every 4 months directly through bank transfer (DBT).
Who can get it (Eligibility): Small and marginal landholder farmer families with cultivable land in their name.
How to apply: Apply online on the official PM-KISAN portal or visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or Village Administrative Officer (VAO).
Official portal: pmkisan.gov.in Visit Official Website
Farm Loans • Central Govt RBI & NABARD

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Low-interest institutional bank loans for farmers to purchase seeds, fertilizers, fuel, and meet seasonal cultivation expenses without high moneylender rates.

What it helps with: Short-term farm crop loans up to ₹3,00,000 at low effective interest rate (as low as 4% per year with prompt repayment).
Who can get it (Eligibility): All individual farmers, joint cultivators, tenant farmers, and sharecroppers.
How to apply: Submit a simplified one-page KCC application form at any commercial or cooperative bank branch or through the PM-KISAN portal.
Official portal: myscheme.gov.in/schemes/kcc Visit Official Website
Crop Insurance • Central Govt Dept of Agriculture

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Complete crop insurance protection to safeguard farmers against financial loss caused by drought, flood, pests, unseasonal rain, and severe weather.

What it helps with: Financial insurance payout directly to your bank account if your harvest is damaged by natural disasters from sowing to post-harvest. Low farmer premium (1.5% to 2%).
Who can get it (Eligibility): All farmers growing notified food crops, oilseeds, and commercial horticultural crops in notified areas.
How to apply: Enroll through your lending bank, village CSC, or directly on the PMFBY national portal before the seasonal cut-off date.
Official portal: pmfby.gov.in Visit Official Website
Soil Health • Central Govt Soil Testing Labs

Soil Health Card Scheme

Free soil testing service provided by the government to tell you the exact nutrients (N, P, K, micro-nutrients) in your field soil.

What it helps with: Gives you a printed soil health report every 3 years with precise fertilizer recommendations, saving you money on unnecessary chemical fertilizer costs.
Who can get it (Eligibility): All farmers owning or cultivating agricultural land in India.
How to apply: Contact your local Block Agricultural Extension Officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) to collect field soil samples for laboratory testing.
Official portal: soilhealth.dac.gov.in Visit Official Website
Farm Equipment Subsidies • Central Govt DBT Mechanization

Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)

Government money help (subsidies) to buy tractors, power tillers, drone sprayers, harvesters, and establish Custom Hiring Centres.

What it helps with: 40% to 50% subsidy on the cost of approved farm equipment and machinery for individual farmers, and up to 80% for farmer groups.
Who can get it (Eligibility): Small, marginal, SC/ST, and women farmers are given priority for equipment subsidy benefits.
How to apply: Register online on the Agrimachinery portal (DBT in Agricultural Mechanization) with land documents (Patta/Chitta) and bank passbook.
Official portal: agrimachinery.nic.in Visit Official Website
Electronic Mandi • Central Govt APMC Trading

National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)

Online trading platform uniting government APMC mandis across India so farmers can sell produce to buyers outside their local district for better prices.

What it helps with: Free crop quality assaying at mandi gate, transparent electronic auctions, and direct online bank payments without middleman commission deductions.
Who can get it (Eligibility): Any farmer who brings produce to a connected e-NAM regulated market mandi.
How to apply: Register with your photo ID, land details, and bank account on the e-NAM mobile app or at the mandi gate registration counter.
Official portal: enam.gov.in Visit Official Website
State Schemes • Govt of Tamil Nadu TN Dept of Agriculture

Tamil Nadu Agrisnet & Uzhavan Welfare Schemes

Tamil Nadu state agriculture department portal providing subsidized certified seeds, micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler), and farm machinery schemes.

What it helps with: Up to 100% subsidy on drip irrigation for small/marginal farmers, subsidized bio-fertilizers, solar pumps, and Uzhavan welfare benefits.
Who can get it (Eligibility): Farmers cultivating agricultural land in Tamil Nadu districts with valid Patta/Chitta and Aadhaar linkage.
How to apply: Register online through TN Agrisnet portal or install the official 'Uzhavan App' (உழவன் செயலி) on your smartphone, or visit your local Block Agricultural Extension Centre (Velaanmai Valaga Maiyam).
Official portal: tnagrisnet.tn.gov.in Visit Official Website
Farmer Advisory • Toll-Free Help Govt of India

Kisan Call Centre & National Farmer Portal

Free 24/7 telephone helpline and information portal where agricultural experts answer farmer questions in local languages (including Tamil, Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam, Hindi, Marathi, Punjabi).

What it helps with: Free advice on crop diseases, pest control, weather advisories, current mandi prices, and government scheme guidance.
Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-180-1551 (Toll-free, 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day)
Who can use it: Open to all farmers across India free of charge.
Official portal: farmer.gov.in Visit Official Website
